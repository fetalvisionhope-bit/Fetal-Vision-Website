3D, 4D Ultrasonography and other fetal medicine procedures

Our diagnostic scan suites are equipped with state-of-the-art GE Healthcare Voluson volumetric transducers to perform targeted evaluations at every stage of pregnancy. We also offer other invasive and non- invasive procedures for assessment of fetal well being

Facilities Offered:

  • Early Pregnancy / Viability Scan (6-10 Weeks)
  • Nuchal Translucency (NT) & First Trimester Anomaly Scan
  • Targeted Anomaly Scan (Level-II / TIFFA)
  • Fetal Growth, Doppler & Amniotic Fluid Assessment
  • Fetal Echocardiography
  • Fetal Neurosonography
  • Amniocentesis
  • Chorionic Villus Sampling
  • Dual-marker Test
  • Quadruple-marker test
  • Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing(NIPT)
  • Fetal Reduction
